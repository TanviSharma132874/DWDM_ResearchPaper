# SinusCare: Literature Resources

Shared reading list for our IEEE conference paper *SinusCare: An Uncertainty-Aware Adaptive Machine Learning Framework for Personalized Sinusitis Risk Assessment*.

**20 papers**, grouped by theme. Source: Consensus search export (Oct 2026). Full author lists, abstracts and the sign-off columns are in [`https://github.com/TanviSharma132874/DWDM_ResearchPaper/blob/main/Research_content/Papers.csv`](papers.csv).

## How to use this list

- Open each paper from its link and read it yourself before citing. Summaries here are metadata, not evidence.
- Do not copy claims into the paper from this table. Cite the original paper.
- Add your name and notes in `papers.csv` (columns *Read by* and *Notes*) when you have read a paper.

## Caveats

- Links are built from the DOIs in the Consensus export. Open each one once to confirm it resolves to the right paper.
- Preprints (arXiv/medRxiv) are not peer reviewed. Cite the published version where one exists.
- *Bingham & Arussy (2026)* has a DOI prefix (`10.64898`) I could not recognise, so treat that link as unconfirmed.
- *von Kleist et al.* is listed as JMLR, but the DOI in the export is the arXiv one. Get the JMLR volume and pages from jmlr.org for the reference list.
- *Güney et al.* is an ICML 2025 paper (PMLR 267:20748-20765); the export had no DOI, so the link is the PMLR page.
- *Yang et al.* is dated 2021 by Consensus (online-first); the print issue is Laryngoscope 2022;132(11):2103-2110.
- *Vani et al. (2025)*: very high accuracy claim on MIMIC-III with deep learning. Weak evidence for our purposes; read critically.
- This list has no conformal-prediction, longitudinal-recurrence or sinusitis-dataset papers yet, and no IEEE Xplore papers. Those searches are still open.
